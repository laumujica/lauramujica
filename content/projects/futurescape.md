## Narrative decisions
1. Name: Laura rejected names too closely tied to an existing series, explored a wider historical/futuristic theme, and selected “Futurescape” from the conversation. [C1, U1]
2. Repeatable cover language: masthead, descriptor, issue information and markers remain; image, palette and atmosphere change. [I2, I3, I4]
3. Image construction: selected sources, color remapping, texture, masks and composite; AI imagery is one input among sourced photography and original textures. [I5, L1]
4. Presentation: cover series framed as a tangible editorial object. The printed magazines shown are mockups, not evidence of publication. [I6]

## Tool attribution
- ChatGPT: simulated creative director and issue brief exploration. Laura challenged alternatives, selected the name and made final design decisions. [C1, U1]
- Microsoft Bing Image Creator: early generative image exploration. [U1, I6]
- Photoshop: manual image composition, masks, blending, retouching, color and texture. [L1, U1]
- Illustrator: masthead, typography, issue information and supporting elements. [L1, U1]
- After Effects, Premiere Pro, Audition: promotional motion, edit and audio adjustments. [L1]
- Laura: human selection, modification, QA and final approval at every stage. [U1]
