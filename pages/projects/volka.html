Overview

A ceramics studio that needed to feel like a museum piece itself.

Volka Studio is a small-batch ceramics workshop based in Copenhagen, specialising in functional objects with a quiet, sculptural quality. The founders approached me with a clear brief: they didn't want to look "handmade and cosy" — they wanted something precise, confident, and shelf-worthy.

The challenge was building a brand that felt premium without being cold, minimal without being empty, and crafted without leaning on the usual artisan tropes.

Strategy

The idea: precision as a form of care.

Through discovery sessions and competitive analysis, we found that most ceramics brands defaulted to warm earth tones, imperfect textures, and handwritten type — signalling approachability at the cost of prestige. Volka's work was different: exact, considered, almost architectural.

The brand strategy centred on the idea that precision is its own form of warmth — that when you make something exactly right, you're showing respect for the person who'll hold it. This informed every visual decision.

Visual Identity

Logo & mark.

The wordmark uses a custom-spaced version of a geometric serif, with the letterforms optically corrected to feel balanced at all sizes. The "V" was refined into a standalone mark — a precise angle that echoes the rim of a ceramic bowl seen from above.

Color System

A restrained palette with a single warm break.

The palette is near-monochromatic — deep charcoal, off-white, and warm stone — with a single accent: a dusty terracotta used sparingly to anchor warmth without decorating it to death.

Typography

Two voices, one system.

Headlines use a geometric serif — authoritative and architectural. Body copy uses a low-contrast grotesque for readability and modernity. The pairing creates a tension between precision and approachability that mirrors the brand positioning.

Applications

Packaging & collateral.

The brand system was applied across shipping boxes, tissue paper, care cards, and price labels. Every touchpoint uses the same restrained vocabulary — nothing is decorative unless it's functional.

Outcome

The work speaks for itself.

The rebrand launched alongside Volka's first major stockist partnership. Within three months of launch, the studio reported a marked increase in wholesale inquiries and press coverage — attributing both to a more coherent and confident brand presence.
