01 / The premise

A publication I wanted to exist.

Futurescape began as a personal exercise in imagining an editorial space for technology, culture, memory, and the planet. The assignment was fictional; the design decisions and cover artwork were real.

In an early experiment with generative AI, I used ChatGPT as a simulated creative director and a partner for developing issue briefs. I challenged and selected ideas, then built the covers and the visual language that connected them. The numbers, dates, and story titles on the covers belong to that imagined publication.

02 / Naming & identity

A name with room to move.

The exploration moved from futuristic cinema toward a broader idea: a magazine that could look ahead while drawing on history and memory. After considering alternatives in conversation with ChatGPT, I chose Futurescape as the name. Chronicles became the editorial descriptor that links each imagined issue to an ongoing record.

03 / The cover series

Consistency without repetition.

Each cover changes subject, palette, and atmosphere. The masthead, edition marker, information hierarchy, and small utility details give the series continuity. The edition numbers are part of the fictional editorial world, not a claim that 25 issues were published.

Constant
Masthead, descriptor, edition marker, information structure.

Variable
Focal imagery, color, atmosphere, and featured stories.

04 / Editorial architecture

A cover built in layers.

The architecture separates identity, story information, the focal composition, and recurring markers. That separation let the imagery change substantially while preserving a recognizable reading order across the series.

05 / Image construction

From image sources to a composed world.

Generative imagery was one input, alongside sourced photography and original textures. I selected and combined material, then used masks, color remapping, retouching, and layered light in Photoshop to create the final compositions. Illustrator carried the masthead, headlines, issue information, and typographic structure.

✳

Where the decisions stayed human

AI supported early ideation and image exploration, including Microsoft Bing Image Creator. I directed the selection, composition, visual edits, quality checks, and final approval of the covers. The diagram above explains one image construction; it is not a screenshot of the editable source file.

06 / The series today

An identity conceived to continue.

The project resulted in three conceptual covers and a promotional motion piece. I prepared layered artwork for animation and adapted the series using After Effects, Premiere Pro, and Audition. Futurescape remains a self-initiated concept: an editorial world I would like to develop further.
