About me

Hi! I’m Laura — a Senior Designer based in Argentina.

I design visual systems, creative workflows, and digital experiences that help ideas move from concept to execution with clarity, consistency, and less friction.

With a background in brand identity, print, and digital design, I’m focused on improving how creative work gets done — combining human judgment, thoughtful systems, and AI-assisted processes when they genuinely make the work better.

I care about creating solutions that are visually strong, scalable, and built for the real world.

Working Principles

  1. 01 · Context before execution.

    I do my best work when given the time and trust to understand the core problem before jumping into production.

  2. 02 · Foundations over rushing.

    Instead of using AI or systems just to move faster, I build thoughtful foundations first to work sustainably from there.

  3. 03 · Focus over perpetual firefighting.

    Normal project pressure is usually part of the work, but constant, reactive chaos prevents any deep or meaningful process.

  4. 04 · Mutual alignment.

    Great outcomes require active collaboration, honoring the design process while valuing the time, energy, and well-being of the team.

  5. 05 · Structure with flexibility.

    Scope, milestones, and expectations must be clear from day one, while remaining flexible enough to adapt intelligently as the project evolves.
