About me

Honest design
for real people.

Hi! I'm Laura — a Senior Designer based in Argentina.

I design visual systems, creative workflows, and digital experiences that help ideas move from concept to execution with more clarity, consistency, and less friction.

With a background in brand identity, print production, web, and digital design, I’m especially interested in improving how creative work gets done — combining human judgment, thoughtful systems, and AI-assisted processes when they genuinely make the work better.

I care about creating solutions that are not only visually strong, but also practical, scalable, and built to work in the real world.
